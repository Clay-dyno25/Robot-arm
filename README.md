Project Ulysses is a low-cost robotic arm designed to demonstrate the principles of automation and robotics. Its primary function is to perform a repetitive pick-and-place task, moving objects between predetermined locations using servo motors and programmed movements.
The goal of this project is to explore how robotics can be used to automate simple, repetitive tasks, reducing the need for manual operation. It also investigates the reliability and practicality of using an affordable robotic system for basic automation.
Beyond building a functional robotic arm, this project aims to provide a hands-on exploration of mechanical design, electronics, programming and the role of automation in modern engineering.

How to assemble:
You will need: 
standard 2mm screws (they come with any mricro servo) x8, 5mm screw x4, 4mmx13mmx4mm bearing x1 (optional)
and an electronics screw driver (like a regular screw driver but with smaller bits)

Step 0 (servo bundles):
screw in the 3 servos (two MG90S's and one SG90) to their respective cases, these will be your 3 servo bundles.

Step 1 (the base):
attach the bearing to the stud poking out the gear plate (the plate with the gears on the side) and fit on the rotating plate

Step 2 (the base part 2):
get the servo case with the hole in the middle, align it with the hole in the rotating plate and screw in your 5mm screw and then place and screw in the servo to the case.

Step 3 (the lower arm):
attach the lower arm (the long part without a pin sticking out) to the servo output shaft (make sure its the side that isnt notched) then screw in the lower arm to the servo with the 2mm screws.

For the other side of the servo case with the pin at the back, simply attach the lower arm to the pin, no screws needed, but optionally (if you don't trust me) for additional support you can screw them together in the screw holes on the pin.

step 4. (the upper arm ):
attach the second servo to the second servo case with the screw holes provided, connect the servo's output shaft and the pin on the case to the opposite holes in the junction between the upper and lower arm, for the servo output shaft screw together the junction and the servo with the 2mm screw. for the other side with the pin, just attach the hole to the pin, no screw needed but you can screw them in optionally.

Step 5. (the wrist):
Attach the last servo bundle (the one with the SG90) with the respective pin holes on the upper arm where the notches are on the buttom side (beneath the side with the rounded corners). again, no screws needed.
